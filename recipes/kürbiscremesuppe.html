Die Kürbiscremesuppe

Description

This velvety pumpkin cream soup made from Hokkaido pumpkin is the ultimate comfort food for crisp autumn and winter days. Gently roasting the vegetables before simmering brings out a rich, slightly nutty sweetness.

Finished with a splash of heavy cream, toasted pumpkin seeds, and a drizzle of pumpkin seed oil, this simple recipe turns everyday ingredients into a restaurant-worthy starter or main dish.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg Hokkaido pumpkin (seeded and cubed)
  • 2 floury potatoes (peeled and cubed)
  • 1 large onion (finely chopped)
  • 2 cloves of garlic (minced)
  • 2 tbsp butter or vegetable oil
  • 800 ml vegetable broth
  • 150 ml heavy cream
  • Salt, freshly ground black pepper, and a pinch of nutmeg
  • Toasted pumpkin seeds and pumpkin seed oil for garnish

Steps

  1. Wash the Hokkaido pumpkin, cut it in half, scoop out the seeds, and dice the flesh (skin on) into 2 cm cubes.
  2. Melt the butter in a large pot over medium heat and sauté the chopped onion and garlic until translucent.
  3. Add the cubed pumpkin and potatoes, stir for 2–3 minutes, and pour in the vegetable broth.
  4. Cover and simmer gently for about 20 minutes until all vegetables are fork-tender.
  5. Remove from heat, stir in the heavy cream, and blend with an immersion blender until completely smooth.
  6. Season to taste with salt, pepper, and nutmeg, then serve hot garnished with pumpkin seeds and a few drops of pumpkin seed oil.
