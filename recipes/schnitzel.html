Das Wiener-Schnitzel

Description

Original Wiener Schnitzel is the undisputed classic of Austrian cuisine. It is made from thinly pounded veal cutlets coated in flour, beaten eggs, and breadcrumbs, then pan-fried in clarified butter until crisp and golden brown.

Gently swirling the pan during frying allows the breading to puff up into its signature airy crust while keeping the meat tender and juicy inside. It is traditionally served with a fresh lemon wedge alongside parsley potatoes or Austrian potato salad.
