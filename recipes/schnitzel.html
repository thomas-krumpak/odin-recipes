Das Wiener-Schnitzel

Description

Original Wiener Schnitzel is the undisputed classic of Austrian cuisine. It is made from thinly pounded veal cutlets coated in flour, beaten eggs, and breadcrumbs, then pan-fried in clarified butter until crisp and golden brown.

Gently swirling the pan during frying allows the breading to puff up into its signature airy crust while keeping the meat tender and juicy inside. It is traditionally served with a fresh lemon wedge alongside parsley potatoes or Austrian potato salad.

Ingredients

  • 4 veal cutlets (about 150 g each, pounded thin)
  • Salt and freshly ground black pepper
  • 100 g all-purpose flour
  • 2–3 large eggs (beaten)
  • 150 g breadcrumbs
  • 200 g clarified butter (or neutral cooking oil) for frying
  • 1 organic lemon (cut into wedges, for serving)

Steps

  1. Place the veal cutlets between two sheets of plastic wrap and gently pound them to an even thickness of about 4–5 mm, then season both sides with salt and pepper.
  2. Set up three shallow dishes for breading: put the flour in the first, the beaten eggs in the second, and the breadcrumbs in the third.
  3. Dredge each cutlet first in flour (shaking off any excess), then dip completely into the beaten egg, and finally coat loosely in breadcrumbs without pressing them down.
  4. Heat a generous amount of clarified butter in a large skillet over medium-high heat until shimmering (about 170 °C / 340 °F), ensuring the cutlets can float freely.
  5. Fry the cutlets for 1.5 to 2 minutes per side until crisp and golden brown, gently swirling the pan continuously so the breading puffs up evenly.
  6. Remove the schnitzels, drain briefly on paper towels, and serve immediately with fresh lemon wedges.
